THE FRAMEWORK

Confidence you can explain

A transparent scoring model converts evidence quality into an actionable tier.

80–100 POINTS

Verified

Current, independent evidence confirms identity, location, and custody.

  • Multiple corroborating sources
  • Recent technical or physical observation
  • No material ownership or location conflicts
45–79 POINTS

Probable

Strong evidence exists, but a field is stale, indirect, or awaiting confirmation.

  • One strong or several weaker sources
  • Recent-but-not-current telemetry
  • Minor metadata conflicts
0–44 POINTS

Unknown

Evidence cannot establish the asset’s current location, identity, or custody.

  • No recent authoritative observation
  • Conflicting or missing records
  • Requires assigned investigation

Confidence formula

score = source quality × freshness+ 10 points when independent sources corroborate− 20 points for a known conflictMaximum score: 100

Source weights: physical audit 45, endpoint 35, network 30, owner confirmation 25, procurement 15, CMDB 10. Evidence loses value as it ages.

Risk formula

risk = uncertainty × criticalityUncertainty = 100 − confidence scoreCriticality multiplier: 1–4Investigation status adds urgency

This keeps the queue focused on assets whose uncertainty creates the greatest security, financial, or operational exposure.

How I would answer the interview question

  1. Normalize the inventory. Establish a canonical asset ID and reconcile duplicates across systems.
  2. Collect evidence. Compare the CMDB with endpoint telemetry, network discovery, procurement, assignment records, and physical audits.
  3. Score confidence. Classify every asset as Verified, Probable, or Unknown using consistent, explainable rules.
  4. Prioritize remediation. Focus first on unknown assets with high security, financial, or operational impact.
  5. Close the loop. Update the source of truth and preserve an audit trail so the same uncertainty does not recur.
